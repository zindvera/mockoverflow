Privacy Policy

At MockOverflow.online, we respect your privacy and process all interactive visual media locally on your device.

1. Camera & Screen Capture Privacy

Our camera preview feature uses your device's web browser to overlay poster or sticker designs in real time. All video feeds and screen snapshots remain local to your device. MockOverflow does not record, stream, transmit, or store video feeds or captured preview images on any external server.

2. Local Browser Storage & Cookies

We use standard browser cookies and localStorage purely to remember your preferences—such as whether you have dismissed our user guide tooltips (posterTooltipSeen and mo_hide_tooltips). We do not use persistent tracking cookies or collect personal identification profiles.

3. External Stores & Affiliate Link Tracking

MockOverflow operates as an affiliate-supported visualization service. When you click destination buttons ("Get This Poster" or "Get This Sticker"), you are directed to third-party marketplaces. These links contain standard affiliate parameters that allow partner stores to identify MockOverflow as the referral source. No personal camera data or private user information is ever shared with external stores.

4. Information Security

MockOverflow does not require user accounts, passwords, or payment information. Because we operate an anonymous, client-side preview engine, your personal usage data remains private to your local browsing session.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at contact@mockoverflow.online.
