Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how MockOverflow.online uses cookies and local browser storage to provide a smooth, uninterrupted user experience.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and local storage (localStorage) are small text files or key-value pairs stored directly in your browser by websites you visit. They allow websites to remember your preferences so you don't have to re-enter settings every time you switch pages.

2. What We Store & Why

MockOverflow maintains a strict minimal data policy. We do not use persistent tracking cookies, cross-site behavioral cookies, or user profile analytics. We only store essential UI preferences:

Storage Key Type Purpose
posterTooltipSeen Cookie Remembers if you dismissed the camera gesture guide so it doesn't pop up again.
mo_hide_tooltips LocalStorage Remembers if you dismissed the form helper tooltips on the home generator page.

No Personal Tracking: None of our local browser storage keys contain your name, email, location, or private browsing history. They exist solely to manage interface tooltips.

3. Third-Party Marketplace Links

When you click "Get This Item" to visit partner stores (such as Etsy, Zazzle, or Redbubble), those external websites may place their own referral cookies to attribute the sales referral to MockOverflow. These third-party cookies are governed by the respective privacy policies of those marketplaces.

4. How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You can clear or disable cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings. However, doing so will cause tutorial tooltips to appear again during your next visit.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our Cookie Policy, please reach out to us at contact@mockoverflow.online.
